Add it to Claude

  1. Settings, then Connectors, then Add custom connector.
  2. Name it Gobbler and paste https://gobbler.build40for40.com/mcp
  3. Click Connect and sign in with Google. Google says the app isn’t verified yet: click Advanced, then continue.
  4. In a new chat, switch Gobbler on and say Let’s Gobble.

Add it to ChatGPT

  1. Settings, then Apps and Connectors, then Advanced, and turn on Developer mode.
  2. Create an app with the same address, choose OAuth, and sign in with Google.
  3. In a new chat, pick Gobbler and say Let’s Gobble.

Just looking? Try https://gobbler.build40for40.com/demo/mcp with made-up contacts.
