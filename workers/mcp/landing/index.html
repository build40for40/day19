Gobbler 🦃

Never forget anyone, but only remember who you care about.

Your contacts are full of people you no longer know, and a few you never properly thanked. Gobbler shows you five a day inside Claude or ChatGPT. Say what you're grateful for to keep them, or let them go. We save a copy of everyone first, so nobody is ever truly lost.

Add it to Claude

  1. Settings, then Connectors, then Add custom connector.
  2. Name it Gobbler and paste https://gobbler.build40for40.com/mcp
  3. Click Connect and sign in with Google. Google says the app isn't verified yet: click Advanced, then continue.
  4. In a new chat, switch Gobbler on and say let's clean up my contacts.

Add it to ChatGPT

  1. Settings, then Apps and Connectors, then Advanced, and turn on Developer mode.
  2. Create an app with the same address, choose OAuth, and sign in with Google.
  3. In a new chat, pick Gobbler and ask it to start.

Want a go without signing in? Use https://gobbler.build40for40.com/demo/mcp for made-up contacts.
